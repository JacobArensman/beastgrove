Description

Beastgrove is a fast survivor-style roguelite where you play as one small monster in a world full of them. Hordes of wild beasts close in from every side; your attacks fire automatically, so you move, dodge and decide how to grow. Every beast you defeat leaves its soul behind. Absorb souls to learn abilities and evolve into stronger forms. Rest at bonfires to save, and if you fall, fight your way back to reclaim the souls you dropped.

Features

  • One-thumb auto-battle horde survival
  • Absorb souls, unlock abilities, evolve your monster
  • Many creature forms to discover and master
  • Bonfire checkpoints and soul drops – every run matters
  • Giant boss guardians
  • Retro green pixel art with a classic handheld feel
  • Fully offline, no ads, no account
